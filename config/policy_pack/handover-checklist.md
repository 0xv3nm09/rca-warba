# Handover checklist (policy pack)

A transfer may close only when:

1. Every open commitment has a named owner and a due date.
2. Every commitment has been accepted by the incoming RM (or returned with a reason and reassigned).
3. No unresolved material conflict or escalation remains on the client file.
4. No facility expires within 30 days without an owned commitment.
5. The outgoing RM has answered the gap questions (answers are saved as recollection, never as verified facts).

# Definitions

- **Verified**: supported by a source span that passes number and entailment checks.
- **Recollection**: an answer given by the outgoing RM; always labelled, never promoted automatically.
- **Material**: facilities, commitments and decisions. Material facts below 0.90 confidence escalate.
- **Signatory**: signing authority comes only from the mandate record, never from a job title.
